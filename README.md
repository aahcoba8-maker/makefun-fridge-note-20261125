[Notes From a Passive-Aggressive Fridge](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-notes-from-a-passive-aggressive-fridge-83c00ad7).

`main.py` writes one funny passive-aggressive note a smart fridge leaves
for its owner to `entry.txt` — a single line, dry, petty and specific, the
sort of thing a smug appliance would put on its door screen.

## The note

> Your yogurt expired Tuesday. I moved it to the front. You reached past it for the cake. I have notes.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.txt` (101 characters, one line, inside the kit gate of 1–200).
